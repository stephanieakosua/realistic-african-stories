# Single shot production template

## Scene [S], Shot [S.N] — [3–6 seconds]

**Shot list:** [size] | [angle] | [lens] | [STATIC or exact single motion] | [ONE physical action] | [one dialogue line or NONE].

### Image prompt (copy-ready and standalone)

Premium photorealistic Nigerian or culturally relevant African cinema. **LOCATION LOCK:** [exact location reference filename, or description if no reference; complete unchanging architectural geometry, furniture, door/window positions, surfaces, objects, time and light direction]. **VISIBLE CHARACTER 1 IDENTITY LOCK:** [name, exact reference filename or provisional anchor, age, skin, oval/round/square face, brow, eye, nose, mouth, hair, distinctive marks, build]; **WARDROBE:** [exact garment and COLOUR]; **POSITION:** [LEFT/RIGHT/CENTRE], facing [direction], eyes toward [object]. **VISIBLE CHARACTER 2 IDENTITY LOCK** (if present): [repeat complete description, filename, clothing colour, position, expression]. [Repeat for all visible characters, maximum three across entire film]. **START FRAME:** [visible instant just before the action, precise props and body pose]. Camera [size, height, angle and focal length]; landscape 16:9; controlled lighting, natural skin, no extra people, no altered faces, clothes, location geometry, text, logos or watermarks. If reference photos exist, attach them for the shot.

### Video prompt (begins from exact approved image)

SHOT [S.N], [N] seconds. [Camera static or one justified motion.] [Only one visible physical action.] ONLY SPEAKER: [person identified by FACE TAG, CLOTHING COLOUR, TYPE and FRAME POSITION], in [fixed voice/accent], says exactly: "[<=10 words and <=100 spoken characters]". ALL OTHERS: [exact silent listeners by colour and frame position] keep lips CLOSED, listen and do not speak. [Natural sound, restrained movement, correct lip sync, continuity and cut.] Dialogue count: [N].

For silent shots: `DIALOGUE: NONE (0 characters)`; no mouth speech animation. End scenes on silent visual pictures. Verify <=3 characters and <=3 locations for the whole film; >=half of shots per scene static and >=2 silent listening cutaways per dialogue scene where possible.
