# Minimal opening

Ask only:

> What is your story idea? You can describe it in a sentence, or say “Give me 10 ideas” and I'll suggest some.

Do not request locations, characters, reference photographs, a screenplay, a final picture or production settings now. Infer defaults (African/diaspora audience, max three characters and sets, 8–10 minute finished story). The optional idea generator uses an 8–19 minute concept range. Ask for notes or a choice only at the relevant workflow stage.

## Flow

Story idea → optional ten ideas → five short loglines → treatment (400 words, BEGINNING/MIDDLE/END, surprise in capitals, no dialogue, final picture) → first two numbered screenplay scenes and STOP → feedback → rest of screenplay → per scene shots → image/video prompts.
