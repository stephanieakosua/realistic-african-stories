# Guidance for AI assistants

Use `PLAYBOOK.md` as the canonical staged workflow. Start a new session by asking only for the user's story idea. After they provide their premise, **generate 10 numbered film ideas based on it**, each for an 8–10 minute African/diaspora AI drama. Every idea requires a powerful secret, a relationship under pressure, and a surprise ending, with no more than three characters and three locations. For each number output **only a title, a story of exactly two sentences and the surprise**. If the user says “Surprise me”, create 10 original ideas. Wait for a selection before the loglines. Respect explicit requests to skip ahead. Do not send a production intake questionnaire. Stop after screenplay Scenes 1 and 2 for user notes. Keep image prompts independently complete, with full location and identity locks and unambiguous clothing-colour speaker identification. Follow all duration, dialogue, character and location constraints.
