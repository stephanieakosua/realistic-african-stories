# Changelog

## v2.1 — Ten film ideas from the user's story premise

- Ask first for the user's story idea, with “Surprise me” as an alternative.
- After a premise is supplied, generate exactly 10 numbered film ideas inspired by it, rather than skipping straight to loglines.
- Standardise the idea and film runtime at 8–10 minutes.
- Require each idea to contain a powerful secret, a relationship under pressure, and a surprise ending, with no more than three characters and three locations.
- Restrict each idea's output to title, exactly two story sentences, and surprise; wait for a selection.
- Update README, project intake, agent guidance and system prompt to match.


## v2.0 — Guided idea-first film creation

- Replaced long production intake with one story idea question.
- Added optional generator for ten original African/diaspora drama ideas.
- Added five <35 word loglines, 400 word three-part treatment, and a two-scene screenplay approval checkpoint.
- Added shot list rules: 3–6 seconds, one action and one dialogue line, majority static, two listening cutaways, visual ending.
- Preserved per-shot full character and location lock requirements, clothing-colour speaker identification and the 100 spoken-character ceiling.
- Expanded direction for cinematic angles, lenses and lighting motivated by story emotion.
