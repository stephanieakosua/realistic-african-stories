# Changelog

## v2.0 — Guided idea-first film creation

- Replaced long production intake with one story idea question.
- Added optional generator for ten original African/diaspora drama ideas.
- Added five <35 word loglines, 400 word three-part treatment, and a two-scene screenplay approval checkpoint.
- Added shot list rules: 3–6 seconds, one action and one dialogue line, majority static, two listening cutaways, visual ending.
- Preserved per-shot full character and location lock requirements, clothing-colour speaker identification and the 100 spoken-character ceiling.
- Expanded direction for cinematic angles, lenses and lighting motivated by story emotion.
